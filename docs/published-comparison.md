# Post hoc visual comparison

On 12 October 2026, after the independent B12 and V11 masks and observation
windows were fixed and their outputs calculated, manuscript Figures 2 and 5
were inspected solely to assess agreement. No annotation, acquisition domain,
scene-selection rule, threshold, or observation window was changed using
these reference images. The references are not analysis inputs and are not
included in the repository or report.

The generated B12 central-reach footprint does not establish the parent/new
channel relationship shown in published Figure 2a. The publication displays
the labeled parent above its disturbed avulsion reach; our 1995-versus-later
interpretation identifies an older southern course and a later northern
course. The reference lacks georeferenced axes, so its display orientation
cannot by itself establish a north/south reversal. The unmatched layout may
reflect orientation, a different relocation phase, or incorrect event
attribution. The present evidence does not resolve the discrepancy. Therefore
the generated B12 maps are partial spectral-method attempts, not a verified
reconstruction of the published event or progradation pattern. No generated
outline was adjusted to resemble the published footprint.

Published Figure 2's duration legend reaches 20 years; the independently
chosen post-relocation window contains only eight years. Its validation
images are labeled 30 November 2007, 2 November 2010, 19 August 2013, and
30 August 2017, with annual counterparts 2007, 2010, 2013, and 2017. Our
already-declared plot years remain 2011, 2013, 2015, and 2017. The 2013 and
2017 annual years coincide, but their domains and independently acquired
composites are not a registered comparison. The exact validation dates are
comparison information only, not new inputs or dates used to select scenes.

Published Figure 5e shows extensive northern and southern disturbance around
the V11 river, whereas the independently drawn polygon covers only a northern
1996--1998 observation window and truncates eastward continuation. This
confirms its partial spatial/temporal scope. Figure 5e labels S_R=14.9,
whereas the article's discussion gives 16.0 for V11. Our brightness diagnostic
is 7.38499, approximately 49.6% and 46.2% of these references, respectively.
Neither comparison is a like-for-like event estimate or a validation score.
