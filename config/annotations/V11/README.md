# Partial independent V11 observation

These polygon vertices were drawn on this project's 30 m UTM grid after
inspection of its own 1986, 1991, 1996, 1998, 2000, 2005, 2010, and 2017
annual RGB composites on 12 October 2026. They are broad manual bounding
polygons around the visible old channel and the disturbed northern corridor.
They are not traced from a published figure, author mask, or measured outcome.

The 1986 parent follows the southern meandering course; the 1996--1998
observations show a northern channel and adjacent exposed surfaces. The new
channel is already visible, with uncertain surrounding disturbance, in the
partly cloudy 1991 composite. A complete channel is visible by 1996. Thus a
precise initiation date is NOT established. There are no acquired 1992--1995
scenes. The disturbance continues across the eastern grid boundary. These
facts prevent treating this as a complete event reconstruction.

Use 1986 for the parent and 1996--1998 for a partial post-relocation footprint.
The unequal windows are deliberate limitations, not a reconstruction of the
paper's comparable-duration windows. Both spectral signatures and all areas
are retained. No exclusion polygon is supplied because no isolated persistent
water body could be confidently attributed inside the polygons; this does not
establish absence of unrelated disturbance. Polygon boundaries are approximate,
at least several 30 m pixels uncertain. The computed ratio is a bounded-domain,
unequal-window diagnostic only. Do not use it as the publication's S_R.

No width, mountain-front distance, local slope, or morphology label has been
supplied. This partial footprint is excluded from Figures 3, 4, and S3.
